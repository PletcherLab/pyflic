# Changelog

## Unreleased

### New features

- **The Program timeline suits the program it shows** (`program_timeline.plot_dfm`; `docs/qc-user-guide.md` 6.8; help topic *Plots catalog*).
  - **Trigger wells are marked.** A trigger well's lane label is bold and gives its threshold (*trigger 20*, every level it has), from `program_timeline.well_thresholds`. A well lit regardless says *On*.
  - **Ticks on older recordings.** On generations 1 and 2 each tick is a trigger well's light coming on, from `OptoCol1`. Under a ratio or fixed-interval Paradigm that is one Activation. Before, these lanes had no ticks, so a progressive-ratio run showed nothing.
  - **The light strip's scale fits the program.** It is 0–100% of a bin when the program lights a well open loop. Otherwise it runs to the DFM's brightest bin, rounded up to 1, 2 or 5 in its decade, and the colour bar says *scaled to this DFM*. Light that follows the fly fills a few percent of a bin, so on a fixed 0–100% scale it was white.

- **The QC Viewer sorts the Feeding Summary and colours the two-well checks** (help topic *QC Viewer*; `docs/qc-user-guide.md` 3.4 and 3.5).
  - **Sorting.** Click a Feeding Summary column header to sort its rows, and again to reverse them. Numbers sort as numbers, and blanks stay last either way. Each Excl. tick stays with its chamber (`_SelectableTableWidget` takes and gives data-frame rows, not screen rows), and the sort is kept across a Params recompute.
  - **Sim. Feeding.** A chamber's row is light red when `Both_pre` is 0.1% or more of its lick samples, and dark red at 1% (`qc_viewer.crosstalk_tiers`).
  - **Bleeding.** Off the diagonal, a cell is light red above the responding well's feeding minimum and dark red above its feeding threshold, the report's warning (`qc_viewer.bleeding_tiers`). The thresholds are the loaded DFM's own.
  - Hovering a coloured cell gives its numbers. The colours exclude nothing.

- **PyMCU's Baseline columns and `Baseline: Adaptive`** (PyMCU `docs/adaptive-baseline-plan.md`, ADR-0004 there; the term *Baseline in force* in `CONTEXT.md`; `docs/firmware-4-data-in-pyflic.md`, *The Baseline columns*). Released PyMCU 4.0.0 writes 102 columns: the 90, then `Baseline1`–`Baseline12`, the baseline PyMCU added to each well's threshold, on every row and every firmware; with `Baseline: Adaptive` it follows each well's resting level through the recording. **Loading:** the columns leave `raw_df` (`paradigm_state.split_baseline_columns`) and live on as `DFM.baseline_in_force`, `(n, 12)`, row for row, through repeated-record drops, range clips and `with_params`; files without them load as before, and the Generation is unchanged. **`Program.txt`:** `Baseline: Adaptive` and its `BaselineRatePerHour:` and `BaselineCapCounts:` are read (`OptoProgram.adaptive_baseline`, `baseline_rate_per_hour`, `baseline_cap_counts`) instead of being skipped with a warning. **The light QC:** the Emulated Trigger takes the row's Baseline in force instead of the first 10 s, and is no longer *approximate* then; the merged cause *a drifting baseline or sustained contact* splits, for files with the columns, into `CAUSE_DRIFTING_BASELINE` (a trigger well resting 10 or more above its Baseline in force) and `CAUSE_SUSTAINED_CONTACT` (resting at it; a fly on the food that the 3-minute running median absorbs); `qc/opto/opto_baselines.csv` (`OPTO_BASELINE_COLUMNS`) gives each well's baseline start, end, range, largest move, changes and, under Adaptive, whether it reached the cap, written only when the columns are there; and a DFM note says which wells moved. Tests: `tests/test_baseline_columns.py`, and `tests/test_adaptive_baseline_sim.py` against PyMCU's simulator run with `--adaptive`.

- **Slices: a part of the recording analysed on its own** (ADR-0020; the term *Slice* in `CONTEXT.md`; for users, the help topic *Slices* and, for users of the older ranged runs, `docs/slices-user-guide.md`). A Design may declare named Slices under `global: slices:`, each with a `start` and an `end` in minutes (`end: end` runs to the end of each recording). A Slice is analysed as if the recording were only those minutes: the rows outside it are dropped at load, before the baseline, the thresholds, event detection and auto-removal. It is the separate ranged analysis `analysis_0_360/` used to be, declared once so a Project can pool it:
  - **Its own exclusions.** Each Slice has its own exclusion group in `remove_chambers.csv` (its name, unless `exclusion_group:` says otherwise), the one the QC Viewer writes to while showing it, and inherits nothing from the whole recording's. `also_exclude:` names groups an analysis excludes as well, never writes, such as one for flies dead from the start; the whole recording takes it too. A Slice may lower or raise the auto-removal cutoffs (`constants:`; `min_untransformed_licks_cutoff`, `exclude_failed_opto_chambers`, `opto_unexplained_fail_fraction`, `opto_unexplained_min_sec`), and nothing else.
  - **Its own outputs**, in `slices/<name>/`: `analysis/`, `qc/`, the experiment report, and `slice.json`, which records the definition it ran with and where each DFM's data ended. The whole recording is unchanged, in `analysis/` and `qc/`. Facets still split each Slice, each window cut to the Slice and one left empty dropped and listed.
  - **Pooled only from the Members that cover it.** A Member covers a Slice when every DFM's recording reaches the Slice's end, within `slice_tolerance_min` (5 minutes). One that does not is left out of that Slice, named in its Combined Analysis (`<project>_SliceCoverage.csv`), its statistics and its Project Report, which are written to `<project>/slices/<name>/`. An open-ended Slice whose pooled Members end more than the tolerance apart is warned of. *Analyze all*, a Batch Run's `run_all_analyses`, `build_combined_analysis` and `project_report` cover the whole recording and every Slice.
  - **The Hub, the scripts and the figures.** The Hub's Selection gains a box per Slice (`omit: slices:`); Run QC, Run analysis and Generate plots, and a script's `run_qc`, `run_analysis` and `run_plots` steps, run the whole recording and every Slice kept. A Slice picker chooses the Slice the Hub shows. A script step takes `slice:` (or the script does). A Plot Spec names the Slice it draws from, and its figure is written to `slices/<name>/figures/`. The Design editor and the Config Editor edit the Slices.
  - **A type with the Ratio analysis (Progressive Ratio, Fixed Ratio) has no Slices**: its Training and Test are split at each group's own training end, which a Slice could clip away.
  - **Script windows are retired.** A step's or script's `start` and `end` are replaced by `slice:`; a step that still states a window is skipped with a log line naming the Slice to declare, because its output overwrote the whole recording's `feeding_summary.csv`. `pyflic lint` lists such scripts, and names, for each old `analysis_<start>_<end>/` folder, the Slice that reproduces it; pyflic still never reads, moves or deletes those folders.
  - **A clipped load keeps its end minute.** Loading a range used to drop the sample exactly at its end; it now keeps it, as every Facet window does, so adjacent Slices share no sample and lose none.

- **A lab builds its own Experiment Types** (ADR-0019; the plan, `docs/experiment-type-definitions-plan.md`, is at commit d0d559e, see [Removed plans](docs/README.md#removed-plans); the terms *Experiment Type*, *Analysis Module* and *Control Comparison* in `CONTEXT.md`; for users, the help topics *Experiment types and chamber layouts* and *Experiment Type Editor*). An Experiment Type is now a definition, not code. pyflic ships three, read-only: Custom, Hedonic and ProgressiveRatio. A lab builds the rest in the new **Experiment Type Editor** (`pyflic-types`, the Hub's Tools panel, or *New type…* beside the type list of the Project design editor and the Config Editor):
  - **A type combines Analysis Modules**: *Progressive Ratio* (chamber groups, Training and Test, the paired fly's light QC, the paired − yoked difference, the breaking point, sucrose persistence), *Hedonic* (the weighted duration summary and its plot), *Paired and yoked* and *Light and dark*. Each states what it needs, and the editor refuses a combination that cannot run. A type also states its layout (single-well, two-well or either), the wells to name, the factor names its Designs declare, its report's plots (built-in Plot Specs, and Plot Specs of its own imported from a Project), and its default facets and cutoffs.
  - **A type states the light it expects**: none, any, or a list of Paradigm types. A recording whose `Program.txt` runs a Paradigm type the type does not expect fails to load, naming it; OpenLoop wells or DFMs and dark intervals are always allowed beside a list. How the light is judged still follows the Paradigms that ran (ADR-0017).
  - **A type may lead its reports with a Control Comparison**: two Roles (Paired against Yoked, or for example Self against Unlit), and a Treatment control, one factor whose control level the Design names with `control_level:`, every other level compared with it.
  - **The data carries its type.** Saving a design or a standalone config whose type is a lab type writes `experiment_type.yaml` beside `project.yaml` or `flic_config.yaml`, so a Project analyses the same on any machine. The lab's library is a folder set in the editor (`~/.config/pyflic/experiment_types` by default; a shared drive shares it). The design editor and the Hub say when a copy differs from the library's and offer to update it, and `pyflic lint` warns of a lab type the data carries no copy of.
  - **The Hub, the Script Editor and the Plot Editor follow the type's modules**, not its name: a module's QC, Analyze and Plots items, its script actions and its Plot Specs are offered to every type that includes it.
  - **What changes for existing configs.** `Hedonic` now means a recording without light: a Hedonic config whose recording has a lit `Program.txt` fails to load, and `pyflic lint` says to build a hedonic type for that light. `ProgressiveRatio` takes ProgressiveRatio and FixedRatio programs; a program with a ClosedLoop Training interval before the ratio Paradigm needs a type that expects ClosedLoop too, under which Training includes that interval as before. Every two-well type now applies `max_med_duration_cutoff` and `max_events_cutoff` when they are set: a Custom two-well design used to ignore them, although the Config Editor showed them.
  - **The Script Editor offers the two-well actions to every two-well design.** It used to treat only Hedonic and Progressive Ratio as two-well, so a Custom two-well config saw the transition matrix and the well comparison flagged.

- **The Hub's QC, Analyze and Plots panels are a list of checkboxes over one button each** (ADR-0018; the plan, `docs/hub-selection-plan.md`, is at commit 2b2360e, see [Removed plans](docs/README.md#removed-plans); for users, the help topic *Analysis Hub*, from *The ticks are configuration*). The two dozen buttons — *Basic analysis*, *QC reports*, *Feeding summary CSV*, *Light summary CSV* and the rest — are gone. Every box starts ticked, and a ticked box is included in every run:
  - **QC** lists the integrity report, the data breaks, the three signal plots, the two-well crosstalk tables, the optogenetic light QC with its figure and the Program timeline, and a Progressive Ratio member's own light QC and its two figures, over **Run QC**. A second card, *View*, keeps **Open QC Viewer**, **View QC plots** and **Open qc folder**.
  - **Analyze** has the **Bin size** box at the top, then the binned summary, the event statistics, the light tables, the paired and yoked comparison, the Progressive Ratio tables, the Hedonic weighted duration summary and the experiment report, over **Run analysis**. The feeding summary, its Facet variant, `summary.txt` and the removed chambers always run, so they have no box; the report is written last.
  - **Plots** lists the feeding summary, Well A vs B, the binned time course and dot plot of the chosen metric (the **Metric** menu sits with them), and the Progressive Ratio and Hedonic figures, over **Generate plots**, which saves each figure into `analysis/` and opens it in a tab, replacing the tab of the same title.
  - **A tick is saved at once, as configuration**, and the log names the file: `global.omit:` in a standalone experiment's `flic_config.yaml`, or in the Project's design for a member, where it applies to every member. What is stored is what is left out, so a config nobody touched runs everything. A script's `run_qc` step and the new `run_analysis` and `run_plots` steps read the same ticks, so a click and a script write the same files. `pyflic lint` warns of an id no run produces.
  - **The panels follow the member.** A group the member cannot have — two-well items on a single-well layout, light items on a recording without light, a type's items under another type — is not shown, and one quiet line says what is not offered. An item the data cannot answer is greyed, its tooltip giving the reason: the Program timeline with no `Program.txt` and no firmware 4.0.0 data, the paired and yoked comparison with no yoked group, the Paired-Yoked Difference with only solo groups.
  - **A run says what it left out** — in the log, and for Run analysis in `summary.txt` — apart from what did not apply. **No run deletes a file**: an item left out keeps what an earlier run wrote.
  - **The Bin size box now sets the binned summary, the report and the binned time course only.** The light figures and the Program timeline are drawn at 10 minutes, and the Progressive Ratio cumulative table and curves at 1 minute, as Basic analysis and the scripts always drew them; the old buttons passed them the box's value.
  - **The Project's Analyze all and a Batch Run are unchanged**: they run each member's QC, Basic analysis and report, whatever the ticks.
- **Each Paradigm is checked against its own rule and summarised by its own measures, under every experiment type** (ADR-0017 and ADR-0015's last addendum; the plan, and where the build departed from it, in `docs/paradigm-analyses-plan.md`, at commit 82aace9 (see [Removed plans](docs/README.md#removed-plans)); the term *Paradigm Family* in `CONTEXT.md`; for researchers, the help topic *Optogenetic experiments* and `docs/qc-user-guide.md` section 7). On a firmware 4.0.0 recording with its `Program.txt`, pyflic groups the six Paradigm types into four **Paradigm Families** — open loop, closed loop (with every Acclimation), fixed length (FixedInterval, and the two ratio types after Acclimation) and non-feeding — and follows the types the program runs, whatever the experiment type:
  - **Every Activation is checked against its Paradigm's rule**, to the millisecond, with the program's settings, one light period at a time. These fail a linkage group, because the firmware keeps each rule exactly, so a miss means the wrong `Program.txt` or a faulty LED: *short activations* (a closed-loop light shorter than its Hold), *max on exceeded*, *wrong activation length* (a fixed-length Activation not lasting `ActivationMs`), *lockout broken* (FixedInterval) and *requirement series wrong* (a ratio Activation meeting a Requirement its series does not give). These warn, because they rest on pyflic's lick detection or PyMCU's timing: *contact without activation* (FixedInterval contact that earned no light), *non-feeding dark*, *interval started late* (each Program Interval's start against `Program.txt`, on every pass) and *DFM restarted*. Each reason names the setting and the value measured. Eight new constants, with fields in the Opto editors: `opto_activation_tolerance_ms` (25), `opto_activation_length_fail_fraction` (0.05), `opto_lockout_fail_fraction` (0.02), `opto_missed_contact_warn_fraction` (0.25), `opto_non_feeding_lit_warn_fraction` (0.10), `opto_non_feeding_min_sec` (30), `opto_non_feeding_dark_warn_fraction` (0.10) and `opto_interval_lag_warn_sec` (5). *Non-feeding light during feeding* now reads the first two of its own, with the defaults it used to borrow, so its results do not change. *Open loop not lit* judges each open-loop window, so one dark Program Interval or one dark pass of a Repeating program shows. `opto_light_qc.csv` gains the checks' columns (`ShortActivations`, `WrongLengthActivations`, `LockoutViolations`, `SeriesMismatches`, `RelightDelayMs`, `IntervalLagSec`, `Restarts` and the rest), and `opto_light_events.csv` each Activation's timing (`OnsetMs`, `EndMs`, `LitMs`, `Overlap`, `K`, …), its `Family` and what ended it (`EndedBy`).
  - **The report shows the rules.** The optogenetic light QC gains a *Rule check* subsection per Paradigm: its rule in words with its settings, a table per linkage group, and a rule figure that puts the measured light against the setting, with the light that breaks the rule in red. The cover's callout counts each flag. The Program timeline hatches wells set `On`, greys wells set `Off`, and marks each DFM restart with a red band. The QC Viewer's Opto Light QC tab shows the checks' columns, and its Program tab lists every Activation.
  - **Light behaviour is reported by family.** The light table gains each family's measures: `MainActivations`, `MedianActivationSec` and `CutOffs` (closed loop); `MaxActivations`, `RewardEfficiency` and `MedianReturnSec` (FixedInterval); `MedianSuppressionSec` (NonFeeding); `LicksPerMinLit`, `LicksPerMinDark` and `LightFeedingIndex` (open loop, over the rows a Program Interval ran). *Light behaviour*, in both reports, has one subsection per family with that family's own headline metrics, in place of the same four for every program, and open loop adds a *Feeding in light and dark* figure. A metric the same in every chamber, such as an open-loop chamber's lit time, is named once instead of being compared. The Plot Editor offers every new column.
  - **Only chambers that earn light are compared on Activations.** Under every type but Progressive Ratio, yoked chambers were pooled into the Activation statistics, which their light only copies. They now keep their lit time beside their partners', and the Activation measures compare Paired, Self and Mutual chambers.
  - **Paired and yoked flies are compared under any experiment type.** Wherever a linkage group lights Yoked chambers with a Paired one, basic analysis writes `analysis/yoked_comparison.csv` (the paired fly's licks, events and median duration minus its yoked partners'), a Project stacks `<project>_YokedComparison.csv`, and the reports gain a *Paired and yoked* subsection, tested against zero and between treatments as Progressive Ratio's difference is.
  - **Progressive Ratio takes designs without yoked controls**, PyMCU's reference design. Chamber Groups come from `Program.txt`'s linkage (two chambers a pair, one a solo group) or from the DFM entry (`paired_chambers`, or `chamber_groups: single`), and the Config Editor's Progressive Ratio card has a *Groups* choice: Pairs, Single chambers or From Program.txt. A solo group's `YokedChamber` is `NA`, and the report says once that the design has no yoked controls. The Fixed Ratio wording is finished (no "a working progressive ratio climbs" or requirement estimate under a fixed ratio), the type's light QC fails a group with *requirement series wrong*, and a DFM restart inside a group's Test window ends it there, the breaking point censored, with a note.
  - **A DFM that restarts mid-recording is found** in the data, an `Index` gap into rows under no instruction, and confirmed by `Messages.csv`: the integrity report lists it under *DFM restarts*, the light QC warns, its gap no longer counts toward the light QC's *lost records*, nor its rows before PyMCU resent the instruction toward *unknown-instruction rows*, and Acclimation, the Activation numbers and the Requirement series start again after it, as the firmware's do.

  Recordings older than firmware 4.0.0 keep their checks and results: sampling the light every 200 ms cannot time a light period. Their report reads differently in two places: the cover's callout counts each flag, and the light QC section names the firmware 4.0.0 checks that did not run, and why.
- **A report for one treatment, or for a run with nothing to plot, reads cleanly.** The first real firmware 4.0.0 recording (a quiet single-well run, every chamber Canton-S) produced a report full of red boxes. Now: a one-treatment design gets one sentence, "One treatment, Canton-S, so nothing is compared between treatments", at the top of Results (and of the Project Report's Statistics) instead of a "no comparison was possible" line under every figure, and no comparison tables; a figure no chamber has a value for (a bout duration when no fly fed) is a grey note saying why, with no caption, instead of "This figure could not be drawn: Faceting variables must have at least one value"; Appendix A leaves such metrics out of its grid and names them; the Program timeline is drawn only for optogenetic experiments, as the Hub button and script action already were; PyMCU's built-in `Idle` Paradigm is labelled as such rather than "type unknown". `compare` with one treatment says so instead of failing in statsmodels.

### Fixed

- **Corrections from the review of the Slices work** (`docs/slices-review-2026-10-29.md`):
  - **Every load of the whole recording that names no exclusion group reads the design's.** A standalone config's `exclusion_group:` was ignored by the Hub and the QC Viewer, which used `general`, but honoured when a run reloaded the whole recording beside a Slice, so the same Run analysis could exclude different chambers depending on the Slice the Hub showed. `load_experiment_yaml` now defaults to the config's group (`general` when it names none); with `exclusion_group=None` it still excludes nothing, but names that group as the one removals go to, so the QC Viewer seeds its ticks from it and **Save Exclusions…** offers it. A design whose `exclusion_group:` is blank no longer turns exclusions off for every member.
  - **QC Viewer, Save Exclusions… asks before writing over another analysis's group.** Typing the own group of the whole recording or of another Slice replaced that group's rows with the current Slice's ticks. It now says how many rows it would replace and waits for **Save**.
  - **The Config Editor no longer drops settings it has no field for.** Saving a standalone config lost `facet_cutoffs`, `facet_labels`, a single-well `well_names`, per-DFM `well_names` and any other `global:` or DFM key without a widget. They are kept; a field you clear is not put back.
  - **The mouse wheel scrolls past menus and number boxes** on the Hub's panels, the Plot Editor, the Config Editor and the Project Design dialog instead of changing them. Over the Batch script menu it used to rewrite `batch.yaml`.
  - **Renaming a Slice renames its own group** when that was still the old name, and its entry in `omit: slices:`. Removals went on being written to the old group, and a left-out Slice came back under its new name.
  - **A Slice's QC and Run analysis record its definition.** Only Basic analysis wrote `slices/<name>/slice.json`, so after the Hub's Run QC or Run analysis the member read "not run". A QC-only run writes the record only where it would not hide an analysis made under another definition, which still reads "re-run needed".
  - **A "not covered" verdict is re-judged when the Slice changes.** A member left out of a Slice stayed "not covered", and out of its Combined Analysis, after the Slice's bounds were edited so that it would cover it. It now reads "re-run needed" until the Slice runs again.
  - **A Slice may set its own unexplained-light failure fraction** (`opto_unexplained_fail_fraction`), the verdict `exclude_failed_opto_chambers` acts on, beside `opto_unexplained_min_sec`. Both editors have a **Fail fraction** column. A Slice's constants are now checked like the design's, and `pyflic lint` and the editors report a `slice_tolerance_min` or `also_exclude` that a load would silently read as the default or as no groups.
  - **An Exclusion Sheet row with no group goes to the member's own group**, its design's `exclusion_group:`, not always `general`. A row whose group nothing excludes (no analysis, no script's `remove_chambers` step) is still written, with its own status, *applied to an unread group*, in the preflight and the log.
  - **Generate plots runs the `run_plots` step itself**, as Run QC and Run analysis run theirs, so a click and a script write the same files. A Slice's figures are titled `… · Slice <name>`, in the Hub's tabs and in a script's figures.
  - **A Plot Spec naming a Slice the design no longer declares** opened on the whole recording and was skipped when rendered. It now falls back to the whole recording, says so above the preview, and is saved that way.
  - **The Project Design dialog refills the form only when a path is finished** (Enter, leaving the field, or **Browse…**), not at every typed folder that happens to be a Project, and no longer carries the last folder's constants.
  - **A Slice's Project statistics keep the Facets' names**, as its members' tables do, instead of minute ranges. A member with no `feeding_summary_facet.csv` beside its summary, which is missing from every Facet's statistics, is now named in them and in the Project Report.
  - `pyflic lint` reads a Project design's lab type from beside `project.yaml`, not from beside the member.
- **Deliberate changes to results, from the same review.** Re-run basic analysis to pick them up:
  - **Generation-1 Progressive Ratio: the light event on the row that clears the training flag is Training's.** It was filed as the first Test event, though the lick that earned it was a Training lick. In MBON02, 46 of 48 groups' first Test event sat exactly one sample after the training end; the Breaking Point falls by one in 45 of its 47 groups with one, `TrainingLightEvents` rises by one (a Training10 group now shows 10, not 9) and `TestLightEvents` falls by one, and `pr_light_events.csv` loses those groups' first Test row. `BreakMin`, `Censored` and the light QC verdicts are unchanged.
  - **A Progressive Ratio group keeps its Breaking Point when only its Yoked chamber is excluded.** The Paired fly's row vanished from `pr_breaking_point.csv`, the still-responding curve and the log-rank test, although the Breaking Point is the Paired chamber's alone. Its paired − yoked difference row is still dropped.
  - **`MeanTimeBtw` and `MedTimeBtw` (and their A/B columns) are `NaN` where no stretch without feeding begins**, as `MeanDuration` already was, instead of 0 s, which entered treatment means and tests as a real value. Facets, bins and Slices change; over the whole recording only a well with no stretch at all does. A stretch is still counted from the recording's start and to its end, as R's FLIC counts it.
  - **A two-well Licks total under `transform_licks` is the root of the summed counts, (A + B)^¼**, not A^¼ + B^¼: the experiment report's Licks dot plot and statistics, the Project statistics and Publication Figures, `bootstrap`, `compare_treatments`, the parameter sensitivity, `compare_configs`, and the binned, moving and dot plots' `total` mode. The paired − yoked lick differences (`dLicksA`, the yoked comparison's) are unchanged, a contrast on the fourth-root scale, and their labels now say "¼-root scale" when the licks were transformed.
  - **What a Slice reads from before its start.** The emulated trigger of the optogenetic light QC (generations 1 and 2) took its baseline from the Slice's own first ten seconds, which changed `ContactSec`, `ContactWithoutActivitySec` and `LikelyCause` with no note; it now reads the run's first ten seconds. Light already on at a Slice's first row is no longer judged unexplained for its first Hold, and it no longer counts as an onset (`LightOnsets`), while an Activation that began on that row now counts. A Slice's `binned_feeding_summary.csv` no longer starts with empty bins from minute 0, and its unfaceted rows' `StartMin`/`EndMin` carry the Slice's minutes, not `0, 0`.
  - **Generation-1 training is read before a range is clipped.** A range starting after a group's training end (the QC Viewer's range, or the API's `range_minutes`) found no training flag and auto-removed the group.
  - **Progressive Ratio's *No increasing trend* warning and the increment estimate read only the Test window's events** when `pr_test_window_min` caps it, as the ledger does.
  - **Smaller corrections:** moving-window tables and plots no longer end with windows that overrun the recording; the last Facet's `EndMin` is `inf`, not 0, and `yoked_comparison.csv`'s whole-recording `FacetRange` is `(0, inf)`, not `(0, 0)`; the tidy exports (`duration_data`, `interval_data`) label wells by the DFM's `chamber_sets`, which they read as the default pairs; a binned summary of a range with no bin no longer crashes; a Progressive Ratio summary over a list of windows no longer crashes its cache.
- **An exclusion edit no longer returns the feeding summary from before it.** The feeding-summary disk cache (`.pyflic_cache/`) was keyed by the member's own `flic_config.yaml` and its data, so a change to `remove_chambers.csv`, or to a Project's design, which a member inherits, loaded the summary cached before the change. The key now carries the design's `global:` block, the chambers excluded and the Slice; old entries are not reused.
- **Deliberate changes to firmware 4.0.0 results, from the Paradigm analyses** (above). Re-run basic analysis to pick them up:
  - **`Suppressions` and `LockedOutContactSec` are redefined.** `Suppressions` counted every record in which a NonFeeding light went out, including the end of a `MaxOnMs` period; it is now the light periods the fly's contact ended. On the simulator's NonFeeding recording it fell from 419 to 312. `LockedOutContactSec` was feeding while the well's light was off, so a linked partner's light hid the feeding of a fly in its lockout; it is now the fly's lick time while its own lockout runs and its own Activation is not lit.
  - **`AcclimationActivations` and `AcclimationEndMin` follow the Activation ledger.** They read each row's Phase, which misreads the record in which a well leaves Acclimation, and missed an Acclimation that began and ended inside one record. They now count the Activations themselves, so `Activations` equals `AcclimationActivations` plus `MainActivations` everywhere, and such an Acclimation has its end; on the simulator's stress recording 8 wells changed.
  - ***Firmware events disagree* counts like with like.** pyflic's count of feeding events included those that started while the well's own Activation was lit, which the firmware never counts as Events, so healthy groups could warn. They are now left out, apart from the one in the onset row, which met the Requirement.
  - **Progressive Ratio reads a linkage of non-adjacent pairs as those pairs.** With `paired_chambers` left out, the groups were taken as chambers 1+2, 3+4 and 5+6 whatever `Program.txt` linked; a program pairing, say, chambers 1 and 4 now gives that pair.
  - **The Progressive Ratio Project report's two red boxes are gone.** "Breaking point by treatment: could not be drawn" and "Could not evaluate the 'x'" came from the breaking-point figures being fed the faceted table instead of their own rows.
- **Up to date with DFM firmware and PyMCU as of 10/17/2026** (DFMV3_V2 024b692, PyMCU 6d1aa42):
  - **Acclimation counts every time the LED comes on.** The firmware now counts an Acclimation Activation each time contact lights the LED, so a fly that comes back within the Event gap after the light went off gets a new one. The test simulator follows it and still matches the compiled firmware. `opto_light_events.csv` leaves `FirmwareEvents` empty for an Acclimation Activation instead of 1, since no Firmware Event earns it.
  - **An exponential progressive ratio no longer crashes or rounds differently from the firmware.** `Paradigm.requirement_for` raised `OverflowError` once e^(Rate·k) passed a float's range, where the firmware gives the Cap. It also rounded in double precision where the firmware uses single, so Scale 5, Rate 0.05 gave 7396 at the 146th Activation instead of the firmware's 7397. It now computes as the firmware does, and matches it over 78,792 checked settings.
  - **`Messages.csv` from the current PyMCU reads correctly.** PyMCU now quotes a message that holds a comma, and writes a message that repeats within 10 minutes once with "(repeated N more times, …)". pyflic kept the quotes in the text and counted such a line once. It now reads both the new file and the old unquoted one, adds a `Repeats` column, and the QC Viewer counts messages with their repeats.

- **Dot plots no longer move values up and down.** The report's dot plots and the feeding-summary grid jittered points vertically as well as across the treatment (plotnine's default), so chambers that all scored zero were drawn between −0.4 and +0.1. Points now spread across the treatment only, as the publication figures already did.
- **A "Write PDF report" script step drew one figure per letter.** The Script Editor saved an unedited list parameter as its default text, `Licks, Events, MedDuration`, and the runner read it letter by letter: 26 figures titled "L over time", "i over time" and so on. The runner now splits a comma-separated value (also for `param_sensitivity` values and `compare` factors), and the Script Editor saves the default as a list.
- **Creating a Project Report no longer floods the console with "findfont: Font family 'Liberation Sans' not found".** The publication figures asked matplotlib for a fallback font list that included faces this machine does not have; matplotlib logs one warning per missing face per text. Only installed faces are listed now.
- **`pyflic config` no longer overwrites the example config in the pyflic folder.**
  - The example `flic_config.yaml` sat at the repository root, so `pyflic config` run there opened it, and saving overwrote it. The repository root also looked like an Experiment Directory. The example is now `examples/flic_config.yaml`.
  - With no folder named, the editor opens the working directory's config only when there is one; otherwise it starts blank, and Save asks where to save.
  - A named folder that does not exist no longer falls back to the working directory's config.
- **`pyflic lint` resolves the chamber layout as the loader does.** It reported "chamber_size must be set" on every config whose layout comes from `chamber_layout:`, its Experiment Type or its Project design, which the loader accepts. It now reports an unknown layout or type, which the loader refuses, and warns when a stated `chamber_size` disagrees with the layout.
- **Filing a loose recording puts its `Program.txt` and `Messages.csv` into `data/`**, where pyflic reads them. They went to `extra_files/`, so the Opto Program was silently lost.
- **The Script Editor edits Project Scripts with the project actions.** On a `project.yaml` its palette and inspector offered only the experiment actions, so project steps could not be added or edited. The editor also offers `facet_csv`, which scripts could run but its catalogue lacked.
- **Clear cache with only a Project open clears its members' caches**, and says how many files it removed from which members. Before, it cleared nothing.
- **Smaller corrections:**
  - The Hedonic type names the files it writes: `treatment_means.csv` and `hedonic_feeding_plot.png`.
  - `bootstrap` defaults to PI on two-well layouts and Licks otherwise, since a single-well layout has no PI. Its mode menu no longer offers `mean_ab`, which it rejects.
  - A `***ALL DFM***` Program.txt no longer reports its own warning line as a skipped line.
  - Generation 2's "no training information" note has one wording everywhere.
  - `pyflic --help` lists every command and entry point, `pyflic-types` included.

- **The Progressive Ratio breaking point is defined** (ADR-0014; terms in `CONTEXT.md`: *Breaking Point*, *Censored*, *Test window*, *Sucrose Persistence*). It is now the classical first-gap rule: the paired fly's lick-backed Test light events before its first pause longer than `pr_break_gap_min` (default 120 minutes), with `BreakMin` the minute of the last one. A lick-free light event neither counts nor ends a pause, and the yoked fly has no breaking point, because its light is its partner's. - **pyflic reads the `Program.txt` PyMCU 4.0.0 exports** (ADR-0015 addendum, ADR-0016; terms in `CONTEXT.md`: *Paradigm*, *Program Interval*, *Hold*). Every DFM section of the new export was dropped before, so a 4.0.0 program was set aside whole. The parser now tells the two grammars apart by content: the 4.0.0 one names each interval's **Paradigm**, lists every Paradigm's type and settings in a `Paradigms:` block, and writes thresholds as a level, `On` or `Off`; the `***Env Mon***` section is ignored, and the `***ALL DFM***` export of a recording made without a Program is read as every well `Off` instead of refused. An old export's five numbers become the Paradigm PyMCU would make of them, named as PyMCU names it (`ClosedLoop-40Hz-8ms-Hold500-MaxOn0`), so both grammars reach the light QC the same way and its verdicts on existing recordings do not change. **`qc/opto/opto_program.csv` changes on every recording:** `Paradigm` is now the Paradigm's name, `ParadigmType` its type as PyMCU spells it, and its settings have a column each (`HoldMs`, `MaxOnMs`, `ActivationMs`, `LockoutMs`, `OnDelayMs`, `EventGapMs`, `Acclimation`, the Requirement series' `Series` … `Ratio`, `NA` where the type has none); an old export's raw `F` and `P` are `LegacyF` and `LegacyP`, and the decoded `AcclimationEvents`, `NonFeeding`, `DecayMs`, `Delay` and `MaxTimeOn` columns are gone. The program table in `summary.txt`, the report and the QC Viewer shows each interval's Hold and its type's own settings.

- **A user guide to quality control**, `docs/qc-user-guide.md` (*Checking your data*): every QC check pyflic runs, for ordinary and optogenetic experiments alike. It covers where each check runs and what it writes; the integrity report, data breaks and the three signal plots; two-well simultaneous feeding and bleeding; the QC Viewer; exclusions by hand and by the design's cutoffs; the optogenetic light QC from `Program.txt` to the exclusion decision; the Progressive Ratio checks; what a Project pools; a checklist and a troubleshooting table. The help gains a *Checking your data* reading path over the QC topics, and the README lists the three user guides. *Getting Started* gains the commands its table lacked (`qc`, `plots`, `batch`, `clear-cache`, `version`) and a first-analysis outline that names `Program.txt`. The Progressive Ratio guide links the QC guide, and adds the two optogenetic settings it lacked and the *Light explained by licks* figure. The help's getting-started topics now say that basic analysis writes the optogenetic light QC, and four descriptions of that figure were corrected: its emulated-trigger panel sits beside the lit-time panel, not below it.
- **The QC Viewer has an Opto Light QC tab** for an optogenetic experiment of any type (the one surface ADR-0015 left for later). One row per linkage group — lit time, the unexplained share and the minute it began, the emulated firmware trigger's contact with no lick, the verdict coloured by tone and the likely cause — opening on the worst group. Selecting a group shows why it got its verdict, its intervals, its light events and its DFM's program, and draws the **Light explained by licks** figure for that group (or the whole DFM). The tab is computed from the loaded experiment rather than read from `qc/opto/`, so a **Params** recompute moves its verdicts at once. **Mark Selected Group Excluded** and **Mark Failed Groups Excluded** tick the chambers on the Feeding Summary and DFM tabs and save nothing; **Save Exclusions…** records the decision. `OptoLightQC.group_lines()` and `plot_dfm(groups=…)` serve it.
- **Every optogenetic experiment gets a light QC, and pyflic reads the MCU's `Program.txt`** (ADR-0015; terms in `CONTEXT.md`: *Opto Program*, *Linkage Group*, *Trigger Well*, *Explained Light*, *Emulated Trigger*). The firmware lights an LED from its own reading of a well; pyflic counts licks afterwards; a drifting well is touched for one and flat for the other. Until now only Progressive Ratio checked for that, and nothing in pyflic knew the thresholds, linkage or decay the firmware used. **The program:** copy the `Program.txt` the MCU exports into the member's `data/`. pyflic reads each DFM's linkage, per-well thresholds and, per interval, frequency, pulse width, decay, delay and max-time-on (decoding the progressive ratio's acclimation count in the frequency and non-feeding activation in the pulse width), classifies every interval's paradigm and lays the intervals out by clock time. It is optional; unknown lines are skipped and listed, an unreadable DFM section is dropped, and more than one `Program.txt` stops the load. **The setting:** `optogenetics: auto | yes | no` under `global:` or the Project design (`auto` by default: a program section or any lit LED), overridable on a DFM's own `dfms:` entry, which stays free inside a Project; `yes` fails a DFM whose LEDs never lit. **The check**, per linkage group, over the whole recording: a lit sample is *explained* when a trigger well has a feeding lick or a tasting sample within the interval's decay plus `opto_decay_tolerance_samples` (2). At least 30% of the lit time unexplained fails the group and 10% warns (`opto_unexplained_fail_fraction`, `opto_unexplained_warn_fraction`, judged from 30 s of unexplained light, `opto_unexplained_min_sec`), with the minute it began; light while every threshold is -1 fails; an open-loop interval lit under 95%, closed-loop feeding that never lit, a group never lit despite feeding, and a program that disagrees with the data's `OptoFreq`, `OptoPW`, `Dark` or start time warn; non-feeding activation is not judged. With a program the firmware's trigger is **emulated** on the raw signal (raw minus the first 10 s, against the threshold) and `LikelyCause` names a drifting baseline or a hardware fault. Without one, linkage is inferred from wells lit together, the decay is `opto_default_decay_ms` (1000) and the check warns but cannot fail. **Failures are flagged, not excluded**, unless `exclude_failed_opto_chambers` is on. **Outputs:** `qc/opto/` (`opto_light_qc.csv`, `opto_light_intervals.csv`, `opto_light_events.csv`, `opto_program.csv`, `opto_light_dfm<id>.png`) from basic analysis and **QC reports**; `OptoLightQC` / `OptoUnexplainedFraction` on every summary row; an *Optogenetic light QC* section in `summary.txt` and in the experiment report (the program table, the verdicts and the **Light explained by licks (QC)** figure) with a cover callout; `<project>_LightQC.csv` now stacks the PR and the optogenetic verdicts with a `Source` column, as do the Stats text and the Project Report. The Hub's QC panel's *Progressive Ratio only* group is now **Optogenetics**, shown for any optogenetic member (**Opto light QC table**, **Light explained by licks (QC)**) and holding the PR checks for that type; new script actions `opto_light_qc` and `plot_opto_light`. The Config Editor has the setting, a per-DFM picker and the thresholds once the experiment is known to be optogenetic; the Project Design dialog has the setting and the thresholds; `pyflic lint` checks all of them and the program. **Progressive Ratio:** `paired_chambers` may be left out when the program names the trigger wells (a disagreement is reported, the config kept), and a light event is lick-free only when no Sucrose Well lick or touch falls within its decay either — new `Explained` column in `pr_light_events.csv`. On the first real dataset no PR verdict changes; the new check fails the three groups whose light ran with no lick, one of which, lit for four hours in training, the PR check passes.
- **Progressive Ratio light QC — did the paired fly earn its light?** (ADR-0013 addendum; terms in `CONTEXT.md`: *Light Event*, *Lick-free Light Event*, *Self-triggered light*, *Resting Level*.) The firmware lights a chamber group from its own reading of the paired Sucrose Well during the run, while pyflic counts licks afterwards from the baselined signal; a Sucrose Well whose resting level creeps up looks continuously touched to the firmware and flat to pyflic, so the light fires on its own and every light-on number describes the sensor. Seen in the first real dataset: one group's light ran unearned for the last hours of the day, another's "completed" training in 13 s without a lick. Every chamber group now gets a verdict over the whole recording — **self-triggered light** (a run of `pr_lick_free_run` = 5 lick-free Test light events) and **implausible training** (training light events, zero sucrose licks) fail it; **no increasing trend** in licks per light event and a **rising** or **elevated** Sucrose Well resting level are warnings. A failed group leaves through auto-removal (`exclude_failed_pr_groups`, on by default) and its reason is in `removed_chambers.csv`. New outputs: `pr_light_qc.csv` (per group), `pr_light_events.csv` (per Test light event — the paired chamber's breaking-point table now carries `MinutesSincePrev`, `LicksSincePrev`, `LickFree`, `RestingLevel`), `LightQC` / `LickFreeLightEvents` columns on every summary row and in `paired_yoked_diff.csv`, a *Progressive ratio light QC* section in `summary.txt`, and two per-DFM figures, **Licks per light event (QC)** and **Sucrose Well resting level (QC)**; the training-aligned traces mark lick-free light events as rings and, like the new figures, keep an auto-removed group in view with its verdict in the strip. The Hub's QC panel gains a *Progressive Ratio only* group (**Light QC table** and the two figures) and the Analyze panel one with **Paired − yoked difference CSV**; the Script Editor has three QC actions (`pr_light_qc`, `plot_pr_light_events`, `plot_pr_resting_level`), the member PDF report a Progressive Ratio section (light QC table and figures first, then the cumulative difference curve and the paired − yoked table, via a new `Experiment.report_sections()` hook), and the Combined Analysis `<project>_LightQC.csv` plus a *Flagged Chamber Groups* block in the Stats text and the Project Report. The thresholds are design constants with fields in the Project Design dialog.
- **A QC subtile in the Experiment group.** The sub-strip is now **QC** · Analyze · Plots · Scripts · AI — QC first, because checking the recording and deciding exclusions precedes the analysis that depends on them. The QC panel holds everything QC for the loaded member, in the order the work happens: **QC reports** writes the per-DFM bundle into `qc/` (integrity report, data breaks, simultaneous-feeding and bleeding matrices for two-well, and the **Raw Signal**, **Baselined**, and **Cumulative Licks** plots); **Open QC Viewer** opens the interactive app on the already-loaded member; **View QC plots** opens the saved signal plots as output-area tabs, one per DFM and kind, reusing tabs on a second look; **Open qc folder** shows the files. The tile's tooltip says whether reports are on disk, and the QC viewer entry left the Tools panel for this, its home. Experiment Scripts gain the matching `run_qc` action. Basic analysis has always skipped QC (`skip_qc=True`) as the slow half of the pipeline; until now nothing in the Hub generated it at all — the plots existed only via the API or the viewer's own load.

### Fixed

- **The Members table's Report column and *View reports* never found a member's report**: they looked for `<member>/<member>_report.pdf`, which nothing writes; both now read `analysis/experiment_report.pdf` (`Project.member_report_path`).
- **The Project Report's cover said "Facets: none (whole recording)" for a Progressive Ratio Project**; it now names Training / Test, split at each chamber group's training end.
- **The feeding summary figure labelled its x axis with an internal column name** (`_RoleGroup`, `_Group`).
- **Auto-removal never ran in the standard pipeline.** `auto_remove_chambers()` — the `constants:` cutoffs and Progressive Ratio's `require_training_complete` — was called only from the QC Viewer's *Auto Filter Chambers*, although the help and ADR-0013 said it ran on every analysis; a never-trained group stayed in every table. `execute_basic_analysis()` now runs it once per loaded experiment, for every Experiment Type, before writing the summary (a second run on the same object keeps the first run's record). The help's `global.constants` page is corrected to match, including the duration and event cutoffs, which apply to Progressive Ratio as well as Hedonic.
- **A QC Viewer recompute or a parameter sweep lost every Progressive Ratio training flag.** `DFM.with_params` re-read the flags from `raw_df`, which had them stripped at load, so every group of the copy "never trained"; the flags now travel with the copy.
- **The Hub's Progressive Ratio curve buttons ignored the Bin size box**; they now use it (scripts keep their own 1-minute default).
- **Saving the Project Design dialog or a standalone config lost constants it had no field for** — dropped, so the type's default came back (a `require_training_complete: false` saved as `true`); both now keep them, and the dialog has fields for Progressive Ratio's.
- **"Starting a Matplotlib GUI outside of the main thread will likely fail" on every run.** Left to choose its own backend, matplotlib picks QtAgg the moment PyQt6 is imported — and then every pyplot figure built on the Hub's worker thread warned, because a QtAgg figure wants a Qt canvas off the GUI thread. No pyflic app shows a figure through pyplot (both Hubs build their own `FigureCanvasQTAgg` to embed one; everything else saves to a file or a buffer), so `pyflic.base` now sets `matplotlib.use("Agg")` beside the import-order rule it already owns: one decision, every entry point, and no GUI to start. That also silences the copies coming from inside plotnine, which draws through pyplot and cannot be fixed from here. Independently, the PDF report's own text and table pages are built from `Figure` rather than `plt.subplots`, the same no-pyplot pattern the DFM QC figures use — a ggplot page still closes through pyplot, since plotnine registered it there. Eleven warnings per report, down to none.
- **The Plot Editor is one panel, and its preview fits.** Content and Style were two tabs, so half the controls shaping the one figure in the preview were always behind a click; they are now one scrolling column of four groups — *Style (shared across plots)*, *This plot*, *Facets*, *Treatments* — the shape PyTrackingAnalysis's editor uses. The **Facets** group hides whole for a time course, which has none. The two lists of the same treatment names (one to show/hide, one to colour) are a single three-column table: original name, printed Label, Colour. **Per-facet sizing is exposed at last** — `facet_width_mm` and `facet_height_mm` have been in `PlotStyle` and honoured by `effective_width_mm`/`effective_height_mm` all along, with no way to set them, so a three-facet figure could only be three squeezed panels in a one-facet width; both read `off` at zero. And the preview is scaled to its pane instead of running off both edges and hiding the axes — scaled down only, never up, because it claims to be what lands in `figures/`; it refits when the splitter moves, from the full-resolution render.
- **The Plot Editor edits one plot at a time.** Its figure list was checkable, so one widget carried two meanings — *which plot am I editing* (the selected row) and *which plots belong to this Project's figure set* (the ticked rows) — and ticking several implied the preview could show several, which it never could. The list is now a **Plot** combo in the toolbar, matching PyTrackingAnalysis's editor, and the figure set is no longer something the editor curates: every figure the Project's data supports is part of it, so `plot_specs.yaml` records *how* a figure is drawn and never *whether* it is. `pubfigures.render_all` follows — it renders every plot the layout and Experiment Type allow, falling back to the default Spec for one the file does not mention (what the Project Report already did), still skipping any whose data is missing. A new **Restore defaults** replaces the reset that unticking used to provide: it discards the current plot's Spec and leaves the shared Styles alone. The check boxes that remain are inside one figure — its facets and its treatments.
- **The Plot Editor previewed nothing.** Two faults, both visible the moment it opened. The Style tab's widgets are the only place `_apply_style` reads a style back *from*, but the style combo is populated with the loading guard set, so the handler that fills them never ran — and `open_target`'s round-trip then harvested them empty, overwriting the Project's style with the spin boxes' minimums: a 20x20 mm figure, previewed as a 94x94 thumbnail, and written over `plot_specs.yaml` on close. The style is now loaded into the form before anything reads it. Separately, a QLabel carries text *or* a pixmap and each setter clears the other, so `_render_preview` setting the empty pixmap *after* the message wiped it: a figure with no data behind it (every time course in a Project with no binned CSVs, every figure in a Project with no Combined Analysis) drew a blank rectangle instead of saying which step was missing. Live re-rendering on every Content and Style edit worked all along and now has something to show. **If you opened the editor before this fix, delete the Project's `plot_specs.yaml`** — it holds the flattened style.
- **`TrainingMinutes` is the Chamber Group's training end, on both its rows.** The yoked chamber's cell was NA — “training was never its behaviour” — which was true of the fly and false of the column: every table and plot that groups by it read the yoked half as missing data. Training end is a property of the *group* (the moment its light stopped being purely closed-loop, which both flies lived through), so `feeding_summary.csv` and `feeding_summary_facet.csv` now carry it on the paired and yoked rows alike, in both Facets. A group that never finished training still has none, and both its rows stay NA; `require_training_complete: true` removes it by default anyway.
- **A Progressive Ratio member's standard plots pooled paired flies with their own yoked controls.** A Treatment in this type names *both* chambers of a Chamber Group, so grouping by Treatment alone drew one cloud of points holding an effect together with the control it is measured against — which averages the two and lands halfway to nothing. The **dot plot** is now the within-group difference: one point is one Chamber Group's paired-minus-yoked value of the chosen metric, panelled by Facet (Training, then Test) with zero drawn as the null, which is the unit `CONTEXT.md` fixes for this type. New `ProgressiveRatioExperiment.paired_yoked_delta()` computes it for *any* metric the summary can express, including the A/B combinations behind `two_well_mode`, rather than only the fixed `d<metric>` columns `paired_yoked_diff.csv` stores; with no time window it differences over each group's own Training/Test Facets, and with an explicit one it uses that single window and says `Facet = Custom`. The **feeding summary**, **binned time course** and **sliding-window** plots — and the bootstrap/sensitivity tables in `analytics`, which resolve their groups the same way — now split every group by role (`w1118 · paired`, `w1118 · yoked`) instead of merging them, because the binned and moving-window treatment tables carry `Group` and `Role` too. `plot_jitter_summary` gained `hline_at` for the zero reference. Project-level faceted publication figures now panel by Role as well (see New features); `proj_PairedYokedDiff.csv` remains the pooled paired-vs-yoked *difference* statement.
- **Saving any matplotlib figure inside a pyflic app could fail with `FT_Render_Glyph … raster overflow`** (first seen writing QC reports for a Progressive Ratio member from the Hub). Not the data and not the thread: matplotlib's wheel carries its own FreeType, statically linked into `ft2font` with its symbols exported, while PyQt6 loads the system `libfreetype.so.6`; two copies of one library in one process bind each other's calls by load order, and the apps imported Qt first, so matplotlib's text renderer ran inside a FreeType it was not built against and failed on the first tick label. `pyflic.base` now imports matplotlib before any app module can import PyQt6, which binds matplotlib to its own FreeType; a test pins the order. Independently, the DFM QC figures (raw, baselined, cumulative licks, binned, moving-window, PI) are now plain `matplotlib.figure.Figure` objects with no pyplot state, so building them in the Hub's worker thread no longer creates a Qt canvas off the GUI thread.
- **The last Facet of every faceted experiment was empty.** `windowing.as_range_minutes` hands the tail window to the loaders as `(start, 0)` — `0` being pyflic's "through the end" sentinel — but the DFM range filters read that `0` literally (`Minutes <= 0`), so `feeding_summary_facet.csv`'s final facet, and any `feeding_summary(range_minutes=(start, 0))`, summed nothing. A new `utils.range_bounds` resolves a non-positive end to the end of the recording at every filter site, and the binned and moving-window tables do the same.
- **Auto-removal now writes `analysis/removed_chambers.csv`.** The Project's aggregated exclusions table has read that file for the `auto` rows since ADR-0005, but nothing ever wrote it; every `auto_remove_chambers()` path (base, Hedonic, Progressive Ratio) now does.

### Fixed

- **`experiment_type` is compared canonically.** A member saying `hedonic` under a design saying `Hedonic` names the same type — the registry is case-insensitive — but design validation compared the strings and called it a fatal deviation.
- **Scaffolding refuses a member name with a path separator**, which would otherwise write a config and a `data/` folder outside the Project.
- **The config editor opened from Member configs… was frozen.** That dialog ran application-modal, so the config editor it launched painted correctly but ignored clicks, keystrokes and the scroll wheel. It is window-modal now, and the editor is raised and focused when it opens. Editing a second member no longer discards the first editor's window.
- **The config editor silently rewrote factorial designs.** A chamber row with some factor columns filled and some blank was compacted by dropping the blanks, so `(blank, Chrim)` was written as `Chrim` — which reads back as the *first* factor, not the second. Factor assignments are positional; a half-filled row is an incomplete assignment, not a shorter one. Such a row is now flagged in the table and left out of the file instead.
- **The twelfth chamber of a single-well DFM could not be reached.** The chamber table neither scrolls nor clips by design, but its minimum height assumed 26px rows against a theme that draws 30, so the last row fell outside the widget. It is measured now.
- **Loading a single-well config left the chamber tables at six rows.** The chamber count was applied only to DFM widgets created during the load, not to the ones already on screen, so the last six assignments of every existing DFM were unreachable until the layout was toggled by hand.
- **The config editor's DFM pane could not be reached.** The top pane handed its (tall) minimum height to the splitter, squeezing the DFM tabs to a couple of hundred pixels with no way to scroll to the rest. Both panes scroll now, and the window sizes itself to the screen rather than to a fixed 1020px.
- **Saving a member config no longer deletes its `scripts:`.** The editor rebuilt the file from its widgets, dropping every key it does not itself edit; it now preserves them.
- **Scaffolding a single-well member gave it six chambers** instead of twelve when the Project had no member to copy from — the blank chamber block follows the design's chamber layout.

### Visual

- **Inline `?` help buttons are quieter.** They rest in a warm grey and take the amber accent (and a faint amber wash) only under the pointer, instead of a column of amber dots down every parameter form.

## 2026-05-04

### New features

- **Subdir batch mode in the Analysis Hub.** A new **"Run 'batch' script in every subdirectory"** checkbox appears in the Project card. When enabled, clicking **Run Script** walks every immediate subdirectory of the project directory, finds YAMLs in each one, and runs the script named `batch` from every YAML that defines it. Each subdirectory is treated as its own independent project, so outputs land inside that subdir. Subdirectories whose YAMLs do not define a `batch` script are skipped with a log message. The Config dropdown, Script picker, and single-project cards (Load, Analyze, Plots, Tools) are hidden in this mode. The two batch modes ("Run action for every YAML config" and "Run 'batch' script in every subdirectory") are mutually exclusive.
